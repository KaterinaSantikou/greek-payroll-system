10) Edge cases & guardrails

Split stacking (e.g., Sunday + Night + OT): show as stacked factors with separate amounts; explain the time band.

Negative/offset nets (recoveries): say “Adjustment from prior period (−€X)”.

Meal voucher split: show exempt vs taxable portion.

Tips policy varies: show whether contributory and the rule reference.

SICK_EFKA: mark as informational (not wages).

Rounding: display exact rounding rule; ensure totals tie out to payslip.

11) Accessibility & localization

WCAG 2.1 AA; keyboard access for all popovers.

ICU format for Greek pluralization and decimal comma.

Use simple language mode toggle (“plain” vs “detailed”).

12) Security & privacy

No IBAN or sensitive IDs in narratives.

Explanations cached per user with RBAC; employees only see their own.

Full audit: payslip_id, ruleset_version, generator_version, hash.

13) Acceptance criteria

For a full monthly run, ≥95% payslips render auto-narratives with coverage ≥95%.

Each amount has a code-level citation; clicking shows the actual formula and rule version used.

Deltas highlight top differences vs prior month correctly.

CSAT widget records scores; 30-day rolling average ≥ 4.5/5 after launch.

Explanations are bilingual and pass WCAG AA; p95 latency within target.

Any unknown code triggers an internal alert and renders a safe fallback.
