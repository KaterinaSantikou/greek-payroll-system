Project Overview
This is a Greek HR & Payroll Management System called "PayrollSync" with comprehensive employee management, payroll calculations, and compliance features.

Core Features & Modules
1. Authentication System
Supabase-based authentication
Email/password login and registration
Social login (Google, Microsoft)
Protected routes and role-based access
Password reset functionality
2. Employee Management
Multi-step employee onboarding form (7 tabs):
Personal Information (Προσωπικά Στοιχεία)
Job Information (Στοιχεία Εργασίας)
Employment Contract (Σύμβαση Εργασίας)
Legal Documents (Νομικά Στοιχεία)
Experience & Education (Εμπειρία & Εκπαίδευση)
Compensation (Μισθός & Παροχές)
Emergency Contacts (Έκτακτη Ανάγκη)
Employee profiles and details
Employee listing with search/filter
Bulk operations
3. Greek Payroll System
Complex Greek tax calculations
EFKA insurance calculations
Collective agreements support
Special hours (overtime, night, Sunday, holiday)
Industry-specific calculations
Multiple employment types support
4. Key Greek Compliance Fields
AFM (Tax ID) - 9 digits
AMKA (Social Security Number) - 11 digits
EFKA registry numbers
Tax Office (ΔΟΥ) assignments
Disability certificates
Work permits for foreign workers
