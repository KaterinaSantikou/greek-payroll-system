Design & UX best practices

Single primary CTA repeated (hero, mid-page, footer).

Above-the-fold load ≤ 2.5s, LCP ≤ 2.5s, no layout shift.

Sticky help (chat/WhatsApp) + calendly demo modal.

Mobile-first: compact hero, stacked trust strip, thumb-reachable CTA.

Bilingual toggle EN/EL (persist selection).

Legal footer with Privacy/Terms and GDPR note.

Tracking & conversion

Events: cta_click:start_free, cta_click:demo, video_watch:percent, section_view:*, form_submit:signup.

Funnels by traffic source; record lead intent (employee count, industry).

Add exit-intent modal (“Get the checklist for Greek payroll compliance”).
