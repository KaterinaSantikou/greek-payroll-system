Dashboard — What to Show
Top Bar (always visible)

Property switcher: Group → Property (Princess, Aegean Suites, Marpunta, The Alex, Atlantis).

Period selector: This month / Payroll period / Custom.

Global search / ⌘K palette: people, runs, filings, actions.

Data freshness stamp: “Updated 2m ago • ERGANI in sync.”

1) Action Inbox (first row, left)

Approvals pending: Overtime, schedule changes, corrections (counts + “Review”).

Exceptions to resolve: Missed/duplicate punches, wrong site, break issues.

Filings due: ERGANI events, APD, ΦΜΥ with D-days and status.

Bank tasks: SEPA file to upload / pain.002 rejects to review.

Goal: 1-click to clear blockers. Sort by urgency & impact.

2) Compliance Strip (first row, right)

Digital Work Card coverage today: % scheduled who clocked in.

ERGANI queue: success rate, fails, retried items.

Min-wage guardrail: employees below floor (count + link).

Rest/48h cap alerts: breaches/at-risk (count + link).

3) Payroll Run Status (period)

Run progress bar: Draft → Validated → Finalized → Posted.

Totals: Gross, Employer contribs, Net, Headcount (Δ vs prior period).

Variance cards: “OT cost +€X vs last month”; “Nights +Y hrs”.

Readiness meter: % exceptions cleared; filings prepared.

4) Hours & Cost KPIs

Regular / OT (Tier1/2) / Night / Sunday / Holiday hours (sparklines).

Labor cost vs budget (Δ € and %).

Cost per occupied room / per cover (for hotels & F&B).

Tip pool (this period): pool total, distribution pending.

5) Time & Attendance Today

Who’s on now: by department.

Late/missing punches: list with CTA to fix.

Devices/geofence health: kiosks online, clock drift flags.

6) Forecast & Risk (next 2 weeks)

Projected payroll vs budget (confidence band).

Employees near OT cap (remaining hours).

Schedule gaps (roles uncovered; suggested swaps).

7) Filings & Payments

ERGANI: submitted / failed / awaiting.

APD (e-EFKA): built / submitted / receipt link.

ΦΜΥ (AADE): built / submitted / payment date.

SEPA: file id, amount, bank status, rejects (if any).

KPI Definitions (quick)

Digital Card Coverage = (# scheduled who clocked in ÷ # scheduled)×100.

ERGANI Success Rate = successful events ÷ total events.

OT Cap Remaining (per employee) = legal cap – YTD legal OT hours.

Night Hours = 22:00–06:00; Sunday/Holiday = calendar flags.

Labor vs Budget = (actual labor € – budget €) / budget €.

LC per Occ. Room = total labor € ÷ occupied rooms (same period).

Tip Pool = configured % of eligible revenue ± manual top-ups.
