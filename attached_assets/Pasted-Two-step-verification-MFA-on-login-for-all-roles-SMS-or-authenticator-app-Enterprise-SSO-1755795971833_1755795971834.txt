Two-step verification (MFA) on login for all roles; SMS or authenticator app.  ￼ ￼
	•	Enterprise SSO (SAML/OIDC) with Okta/AzureAD; SCIM or JIT provisioning for employees.  ￼ ￼ ￼
	•	Self-serve employer sign-up (SMB) or sales-assisted; employee access by invitation link from employer.  ￼ ￼ ￼
	•	Password standards aligned to NIST 800-63B (≥8 chars, no arbitrary complexity, screen against breached lists).  ￼ ￼
	•	TLS-only, anti-enumeration, and session protections per OWASP.  ￼
	•	Clear consent/terms & privacy links at sign-up; GDPR-aware wording (consent is one legal basis, not always required).  ￼ ￼

Best practices to follow (UX + Security)

Sign up (employer/admin)
	•	Friction-light step 1: Email + password (or “Continue with Google/Microsoft”). Defer company/tax details to a post-sign-up wizard.  ￼
	•	NIST-compliant password UI: min 8 chars, breached-password check, show/hide toggle, strength meter; no forced symbols.  ￼
	•	MFA immediately after email verification; offer authenticator app first, SMS second.  ￼
	•	Legal & consent block: “By creating an account you agree to the Terms & Privacy Policy” + separate, specific consent checkboxes only if you rely on consent as the legal basis (e.g., marketing).  ￼ ￼
	•	Greek market: language toggle EL/EN on the page; collect AFM/ΔΟΥ and company legal name after account creation (onboarding), not on the very first screen.

Log in (all users)
	•	Identifier-first flow (email → route to SSO vs native) to support enterprises; hide whether an email exists to reduce enumeration risk.  ￼
	•	MFA every login on risky signals; trusted-device (long-lived) cookies otherwise. Intuit/Gusto make 2-step the norm.  ￼ ￼
	•	SSO buttons for enterprise tenants; Google/Microsoft for SMBs. (Rippling/Workday commonly pair SSO + SCIM.)  ￼ ￼
	•	Account recovery: password reset via email + MFA step-up; staffed proof-of-identity fallback for payroll-admin lockouts (Intuit pattern).  ￼
	•	Security hygiene: TLS only, secure cookies, CSRF on POSTs, rate limiting, reCAPTCHA after failures, generic error copy (“Email or password is incorrect”).  ￼

Employee onboarding access
	•	Invite-based activation (unique, time-boxed link) → set password → MFA → personal/tax/bank info (Gusto pattern).  ￼
	•	Mobile-friendly flow; support passkeys/WebAuthn as an MFA factor for high-risk roles (aligns with modern NIST guidance).  ￼
